## ACT I — THE SETTING (±0:00–0:30) · Scene 01 Aftermath → peta wide
> June, nineteen forty-four. In the forests and marshes of Belarus, the German Army Group Centre holds a long line, deep inside the Soviet Union.
> At its southern end lies Bobruisk: a railway town on the Berezina river, declared a fortress, to be held at all costs.
> Here waits the German Ninth Army. Its commander, General Hans Jordan, has warned that a great Soviet attack is coming. His superiors do not listen.

Sumber: Fester Platz Bobruisk (V), Jordan memperingatkan, ditepis Busch (V).

## ACT II — THE OFFENSIVE BEGINS (±0:30–0:55) · Scene 02 Artillery → peta (garis awal, kilatan artileri)
> On the twenty-fourth of June, the guns of the First Belorussian Front open fire.
> Its commander, Konstantin Rokossovsky, has insisted on a bold plan. Not one attack, but two: one from the north, one from the south, to close around Bobruisk like a pair of jaws.
> In the north, near Rogachev, the first assault is thrown back. So the guns fire again.

Sumber: rencana dua serangan, Stalin awalnya menolak (V); serangan pertama di utara dipukul mundur, persiapan artileri ulang (V).

## ACT III — BREAKTHROUGH AND EXPLOITATION (±0:55–1:20) · Scene 03 Infantry → peta → Scene 04 Armour
> This time, the line breaks. The German 134th Infantry Division collapses, and the Soviet 3rd Army pushes west.
> A breakthrough opens a door. Exploitation is what happens next: Soviet tank corps race through the gap, deep into the German rear.

Sumber: 134th ID kolaps, 3rd Army maju (V); 9th Tank Corps mengeksploitasi terobosan (V, halaman 48th Army).

## ACT IV — THE MARSHES (±1:20–1:45) · Scene 05 Marsh → peta selatan
> In the south, the ground is swamp, and the Germans hold it with fewer, weaker divisions.
> But the Soviet 65th Army crosses it anyway, on roads of logs laid across the marsh.
> Behind them comes a cavalry and mechanised group under Issa Pliyev, cutting west, towards Slutsk.

Sumber: divisi lebih lemah di sektor 9th Army (Dunn, V); jalan balok di rawa (V, halaman Batov); KMG Pliyev ke Slutsk (V).

## ACT V — ENCIRCLEMENT (±1:45–2:10) · peta kantong (zoom) → Scene 06
> The jaws close. By the twenty-seventh of June, the German Thirty-Fifth Corps and much of the 20th Panzer Division are trapped east of the Berezina.
> The pocket is attacked from the air, again and again.
> Jordan's last reserve, the 20th Panzer, was sent north, then turned south. Hitler removes him from command.

Sumber: kantong 27 Jun (V); serangan udara terus-menerus (V); 20th Pz diperintah berbalik (V); Jordan dicopot (V, tanggal 27–28 Jun).
Catatan VO: "Thirty-Fifth Corps" dipakai agar TTS tidak membaca "XXXV" salah; teks layar tetap "XXXV CORPS".

## ACT VI — RETREAT AND COLLAPSE (±2:10–2:35) · peta terobosan → Scene 07
> On the twenty-eighth, the order comes to break out. What is left of the 20th Panzer leads the way, while the 12th Panzer Division attacks from the west to meet them.
> According to one account, when its commander arrived, Ninth Army's chief of staff greeted him with a single sentence: "Good to see you. Ninth Army no longer exists."

Sumber: terobosan dipimpin sisa 20th Pz, 12th Pz menyerang dari barat (V); kutipan: Adair hlm. 135 (**K, satu sumber**, diberi penanda "According to one account").

## ACT VII — THE BATTLE FOR THE TOWN (±2:35–3:00) · Scene 08 Urban → Scene 08b → peta kota
> Inside Bobruisk, one German division, the 383rd Infantry, is ordered to hold the town while the others escape.
> The 65th Army fights its way in, street by street. On the twenty-ninth of June, Bobruisk falls.

Sumber: 383rd ID menahan kota, mundur menjelang fajar 29 Jun; 65th Army merebut kota dari jalan ke jalan (V).

## ACT VIII — CONCLUSION (±3:00–3:30) · peta wide akhir → Scene 01 → hitam
> Only part of the trapped force gets out. Estimates of German losses differ, but tens of thousands were killed or captured in less than a week.
> The Soviet war correspondent Vasily Grossman reached the area soon after. He wrote: "A cauldron of death was boiling here."
> For the Red Army, the road to Minsk was open. For Army Group Centre, this was the start of what historians often call the greatest defeat in German military history.
> Bobruisk. June, nineteen forty-four.

Sumber: angka berbeda antar sumber → "tens of thousands" (semua sumber sepakat); kutipan Grossman: Beevor & Vinogradova, *A Writer at War*, hlm. 273 (V); jalan ke Minsk terbuka (V); "greatest defeat": Frieser 2007 / Glantz & House ("possibly the greatest defeat") → diberi atribusi "historians often call" (I).
