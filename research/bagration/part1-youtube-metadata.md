## Deskripsi
On 22 June 1944, three years to the day after Germany invaded the Soviet Union, the Red Army launched Operation Bagration. Within two weeks, Germany's Army Group Centre lost 25 divisions and some 300,000 men. This is how the trap was set.

Part 1 of 4: the German bulge in Belarus, the Soviet deception (maskirovka), the partisan Rail War, and the opening battles at Vitebsk, Orsha and Mogilev.
